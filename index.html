OUR MANDATE

What We Are Called to Advance

Our mandate is expressed through four foundational objectives that shape our message, discipleship, spiritual culture, and Kingdom assignment.

01

Kingdom Manifestation & Personal Transformation

Growing into Christlikeness and expressing the life of Christ in everyday living. We pursue transformation that makes the reality of Christ visible in character, conduct, purpose, and influence.

02

Soul Winning & Intentional Discipleship

Proclaiming the Gospel, winning souls, and intentionally raising believers into maturity and disciple-makers. We are committed to reaching people and building them in the faith.

03

Cultivation in the Spirit of Truth

Developing wisdom, understanding, revelation, discernment, and a life that walks in truth. We cultivate spiritual depth so believers can know God and respond to His Word accurately.

04

Illumination of Light & Operation in Kingship and Priesthood

Awakening identity, authority, calling, kingship, priesthood, godly influence, and service. We equip believers to represent God’s Kingdom as light in their generation.
